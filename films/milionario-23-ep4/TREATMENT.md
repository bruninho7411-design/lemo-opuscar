## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Price war**: you cut prices too and both bleed. Realistic, but the hero loses agency and the lesson is "whoever has more money wins".
2. **Sabotage thriller**: you investigate Ômega first. The mystery belongs in the cliffhanger, not the body.
3. **Change the game (chosen).** One week later your shop is half empty. Caio says cut the price; the narrator shows why a price war is lost (two money stacks, Ômega's grows). You answer with a different product: pickup and delivery, ready in 24 h, folded, R$ 15 per delivery; Caio on a delivery bike; the phone fills with orders (0 → 800, +R$ 12 mil/mês). The rival closes. Then the twist: you find out who owns Grupo Ômega, someone you know very well (a silhouette with a familiar white moustache).
