## Where we are

EP 5 ended on the letter: Seu Arlindo (Grupo Ômega) bought the building of Lavanderia 1 — "DESOCUPAR EM 30 DIAS".

## The one person

18–30, rents where they live or wants their own business one day, follows money stories on Reels. What they don't know, and
can use tomorrow: the law that protects whoever rents. That is the gap, and it is real (Lei 8.245/1991, the Lei do Inquilinato):

- art. 8: whoever buys a rented property may end the lease, with **90 days** to leave (not 30);
- art. 27: when the owner sells, the tenant has the right to buy first, **at the same price**, and must be told;
- art. 33: a tenant passed over can **deposit the same price** (plus the transfer costs) and take the property, within six months of
  the sale's registration, if the lease was **registered on the property's record (matrícula, cartório de registro de imóveis)
  at least 30 days before the sale**. Registering it only at a títulos-e-documentos office is not enough (IRIB, TJ decisions).

The film simplifies the wording, never the rule. The caption names the articles and says it is not legal advice.

## Three structures

1. **"Ele errou duas vezes."** Caio's panic on the 30 days → you, calm: two mistakes → mistake one (90 days, quick) → mistake two
   is worth R$ 900 mil (loop) → the right to buy first → nobody offered → "pode ser nosso?!" → only if the lease was registered on
   the building's record; almost nobody does it (new loop, ~55%) → follow/save call while it is open → "Registrei no dia que
   assinei." → the deposit → Arlindo: "Ele tomou o meu prédio?!" → the building in your name, rent zero (the gain) → Arlindo:
   "quanto sobrou na sua conta?" (the cliffhanger: EP 7, O caixa).
2. **In medias res.** Open on Arlindo crushing the court order, rewind "29 dias antes". Strong first frame, but the rewind costs
   seconds and the law arrives late; the viewer waits for the premise.
3. **The countdown.** 30 → 0 days, boxes, customers lost, the law found on day 1. The premise is not the content: 20 seconds of
   decline before anything useful.

**Chosen: 1.** Each line sells the next; the first useful fact (90 days, not 30) lands by ~6 s; the second loop opens before the
first one closes; the gain (the building, rent zero) is real and the new threat (cash) comes out of it, not from nowhere.
