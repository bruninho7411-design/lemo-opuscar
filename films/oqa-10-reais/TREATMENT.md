## The one person

Anyone with a father, 16–35, scrolling at night. The gap is a number they can feel: R$10 is nothing, every night for 18 years is
R$ 65.700 (10 × 365 × 18; the film counts 365 nights a year). The counters are the hook (watch the jar grow, wait for 18); the
father's lines are why it gets sent ("manda pro seu pai"); the open question at the end (what if he had invested it?) is why it
gets a follow (part 2).

## Three structures

1. **One night per age, counters running.** Birth → 3 → 7 → the hard month → 13 → 16 → 18: each a single night, the same
   gesture (a R$10 note in the jar), the father aging, one whispered line; at 18 he hands over the box. End: R$ 65.700, "e se ele
   tivesse investido? parte 2", "manda pro seu pai".
2. **Start at 18 and rewind.** The box first, then the nights. Strong image, but it spends the payoff first.
3. **Only the number.** A jar filling in time-lapse with the counter. Clear but cold: nothing to send to a father.

**Chosen: 1.** The counter is legible in the first second and every cut advances it; the hard month at ~60% (the jar kept
filling in a bad month) is the emotional turn before the payoff; the last loop (invested?) opens as the first one closes.
