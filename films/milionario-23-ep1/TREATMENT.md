# TREATMENT — "POV: você ficou milionário aos 23" · EP 1

Style: **Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon** · vertical 1080×1920 · 24 fps · ~66 s · pt-BR voice (VoiceStudio / OmniVoice: the series' young male narrator from `lula-30s` / `terremoto-gato`, plus a designed second voice for the friend) + burned-in captions.
Series: EP 1 of a POV mini-series. Audience: short-form feeds (Shorts / Reels / TikTok). Goals: hold to the end, **follow** (two calls, one mid-film, one on the end card), and come back for EP 2 (cliffhanger).
Requests carried in: on-screen **gains** (every money moment pops a `+R$` tag and a running *patrimônio* counter climbs), a **follow** call inside the film, hooks.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Linear rags-to-riches**: 18 → 19 → 21 → 23 in order. Clear, but the hook ("milionário") would arrive only at the end; feeds punish slow starts.
2. **The list**: "5 coisas que fiz pra ficar milionário aos 23": a counter of habits. Retentive, but it is advice, not a story; no characters, no POV.
3. **Cold open at 23, flash back through the phone, return, cliffhanger (chosen).** We start on the punchline: 23, a cheap sofa, a hoodie, instant noodles, a broke friend asking for R$ 50, and a phone that says R$ 1.000.000. The phone is the travel device: we rush into its glowing screen and swirl into each year (18, 19, 21), each a different palette world, while the gains pile up. Back on the same sofa, the phone buzzes with a big PIX, the friend sees it: freeze, "EP 2". It opens on the hook, uses the style's own grammar (duo contrast, worlds as palettes, swirl wipe, the suspiciously normal world, dead-air reversal) and ends on a question only EP 2 answers.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Aos 23 você tem um milhão — e continua no mesmo sofá, de moletom, emprestando cinquenta pro amigo que não sabe de nada.*

- **Setup (0–11 s):** the beige living room (the suspiciously normal world). Hook text + line in frame 1. The friend asks for money; you, deadpan, say "Claro." The phone glows.
- **Turn (11–55 s):** rush into the phone → 18 (laundromat job, R$ 1.500, the owner who only collects coins: the idea) → 19 (friends spend, you save 80 %) → follow call → 21 (you buy the laundromat from the retiring owner, paid in instalments to him) → the second and third laundromat on a machine-gun beat. The *patrimônio* counter runs to R$ 1.000.000.
- **Ending (55–66 s):** back to the same sofa, same frame as the opening. The phone buzzes: PIX R$ 27.400. The friend's eyes. Dead air. "Mano... que que é isso?" Hard cut to the end card: "EP 2 — o segredo vazou" + follow.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Animated "POV / story" shorts** (the reference the user sent: a deadpan millionaire and a nervous friend on two sofas). Learn: dialogue-driven two-hander, deadpan vs panic, a narrator in second person, short lines, one idea per picture. Don't take: their characters, drawings, story or lines.
- **Adult sitcom cartoons** (grammar only, per STYLE.md): reaction close-ups after every absurd image, cold pauses, a petty goal pursued with big means (here the reverse: huge means, petty life). Don't take: any character, portal design, palette or catchphrase.

## 4. Cast and worlds

- **Você (23)**: lanky, dark-grey hoodie with the hood half up, round glasses, messy brown hair, heavy lids. Deadpan. Absurd costume idea: the same hoodie in every year (it just gets more worn).
- **Caio (the friend)**: short, round, mustard bomber jacket, a fresh fade with one shaved line, big nervous eyes, phone with a cracked screen. Panic energy.
- **Seu Arlindo** (laundromat owner, 18 and 21): old, bald, white moustache, cardigan, a coin pouch.
- **Worlds = years**, one palette each (line and eye white constant; gold glow reserved for the phone/money):
  - **23 / sofa**: beige, khaki, brown (the suspiciously normal world).
  - **18 / laundromat**: teal and coral.
  - **19 / bedroom vs. party**: ultraviolet and mint.
  - **21 / your laundromat**: tomato and sky blue.
- **The device**: your phone. Its screen is the only thing that glows (warm gold, `#ffc93a`), rings and sparks; the gains tags share it, because money is the magic here.
