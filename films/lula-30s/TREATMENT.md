# TREATMENT — "30 Segundos de Plano de Governo"

Style: **Tilt-Shift Miniature** · vertical 1080×1920 · 24 fps · ~52 s · pt-BR voice (Kokoro `pf_dora`) + burned-in captions.
Audience: Gen Z, short-form feeds (Reels / TikTok / Shorts). Goal: retention + comments. Tone: high-energy, playful, politically neutral: each proposal is stated as written in the 2026 programme (FACTS.md), framed as a "what if", never as an outcome.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The checklist** — a split-screen list of 10 proposals ticked one by one over a static city. Clear, but a list with a backdrop; no use of the lens or the clock. Rejected: low motion.
2. **Before/after per proposal** — ten tiny mini-films, each cutting from "normal" to "cumprida". Readable, but 20 cuts in 30 s fragment the place; the viewer never feels *one* country changing.
3. **The 30-second oner (chosen)** — a single Brazilian miniature city. Real time, grey Monday. A countdown starts, the time-lapse clock slams to ×60, and the camera flies down one avenue while every proposal pops into the city, one per 1.5 bars, all accumulating. At 0:00 the tape stops, silence, and the city **rewinds** to the grey Monday. The last frame is the first frame: the video loops. Chosen because it uses the style's native moves (speed drop, band as pointer, scale reveal), gives constant motion, and the loop + closing question are retention/comment engines.

## 2. Logline and arc

*E se todas as propostas do Lula virassem realidade ao mesmo tempo — por só 30 segundos?*

- **Setup (0–6 s):** a grey, real-time city at 6 a.m. Monday; one tiny worker at a bus stop. Hook question on screen in the first frame.
- **Turn (6–36 s):** "Valendo!" — countdown 0:30, ×60 time-lapse. 10 proposals land, the city fills with colour, crowds, new buildings. Last bar: the camera rises; everything at once.
- **Ending (36–52 s):** 0:00 → tape-stop to silence → rewind → the same grey Monday. Voice asks: what comes off the paper? Comment. Loop.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Sam O'Hare, *The Sandpit*** — learn: the high vantage, the band, stuttered time-lapse vs smooth camera. Don't take: any place, shot or music.
- **"What if…" explainer shorts (Kurzgesagt-style hooks, MrBeast-style 30 s countdown stakes)** — learn: hook in frame 1, an on-screen timer as tension device, a counter ("3/10") that keeps people watching to the end, a question to drive comments, a seamless loop. Don't take: characters, graphics, music, branding.

The time-lapse readout says `4 ANOS EM 30 s · ×4.204.800` (a 4-year term compressed into the countdown); the grade goes from grey (sat 0.42) to full colour one step per proposal, and the rewind brings the grey back. ## 6. Cue map

- 0–4.6: no music; real city ambience (traffic hum, birds, a bus brake). 4.6–6.0: riser + reversed cymbal, filtered marimba ostinato sneaks in (J-cut into the drop).
- 6.0–34.1: 128 bpm. Marimba ostinato (Reich-style, two phased lines enter one at a time), glockenspiel on each pop, synth kick + clap + shaker in a light funk-carioca "tamborzão" pattern (Gen Z hook) — tuned percussion leads, drums support. Each proposal adds one instrument layer (gated by the pop). Bar 9 (proposal 6): drums drop for one bar (breath), then return bigger.
- 34.1–36.0: full ensemble, crash at 34.125, sustained chord.
- 36.0: **tape-stop** (varispeed down 0.6 s) into silence; one bird.
- 36.9–40.0: reverse-tape whoosh, granular rewind hiss, ticks.
- 40.0–52.0: music-box version of the ostinato, soft; ducked under VO; ends to match loop (fade so the loop restart lands on the hook).

- Captions on a **Brazilian highway-sign panel** (green `#0b6e3a` with white border, rounded corners), Overpass ExtraBold, white, active word highlighted yellow `#ffd21f` (karaoke for retention), ≤ 2 lines, sitting in the lower blur band (y ≈ 66–76 % of height), never over the sharp band.
