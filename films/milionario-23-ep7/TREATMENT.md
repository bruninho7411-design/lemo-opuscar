## Where we are

EP 6 ended on Arlindo: "900 mil, garoto... quanto sobrou na sua conta?"

## The one person

18–30, saves money, dreams of a business or a property. What they can use: **profit is not cash** (a business that makes money
can still die with an empty till), and **an idle asset is a cost** (two empty floors earn nothing). Both are stated as general
practice, with no legal or statistical claim. Considered and dropped: the IPTU debt that follows a building to its buyer (CTN art.
130): true in general, but a building taken through the courts (EP 6's art. 33) is a grey zone, so the film would teach it wrong.

## Three structures

1. **The balance, the blow, the idle floors.** Phone → app: R$ 41 mil left → Caio: "Quarenta e um mil, mano?!" → four machines
   break the same week, R$ 32 mil → R$ 9 mil left for three shops → "Era isso que ele queria." → profit ≠ cash → but the
   building had two empty floors nobody looked at (new loop, ~55%) → save/follow while it is open → rent both, R$ 6 mil a
   month; the repair in 8 × R$ 4 mil → the building pays the repair and R$ 2 mil is left (the gain) → one tenant signed with a
   gold pen → the chair turns: the new upstairs neighbour is Seu Arlindo (cliffhanger, EP 8 · O inquilino).
2. **Start on the tenant.** Open on the gold pen and the chair turn, rewind. Strong image, but it spends the cliffhanger first.
3. **A day of the till.** Morning to night of a shop with no cash. Real, but slow; the premise is not the content.

**Chosen: 1.** The first number lands in the first second (the app), the blow by ~5 s, the second loop opens before the first
closes, and the cliffhanger echoes EP 5's reveal line on purpose ("... era o Seu Arlindo.").
