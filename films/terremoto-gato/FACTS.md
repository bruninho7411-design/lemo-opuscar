# FACTS — safety claims in the film

| On screen / voice | Source | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| Don't run to the door; drop, take cover, hold on | Ready.gov, Earthquakes — https://www.ready.gov/earthquakes | "If you are inside, stay and do not run outside; avoid doorways." · "Drop where you are onto hands and knees." · "Cover your head and neck with one arm and hand." · "Hold until the shaking stops." |
| Stay away from the window | Ready.gov, Earthquakes | "crawl next to an interior wall (away from windows)" |
| Don't try to grab the cat; it will hide on its own | Seattle OEM pet preparedness — https://seattle.gov/Documents/Departments/Emergency/Preparedness/FamilyPlans/PetPreparedness.pdf ; Carson City EM — https://www.carsoncity.gov/government/departments-a-f/fire-department/divisions/emergency-management-division/earthquake-preparedness/don-t-forget-your-pets ; Found Animals — https://www.foundanimals.org/news/earthquake-safety-pets | "Animals will instinctively protect themselves and hide where they're safe." · "Cats will most likely not tolerate being picked up or held during an earthquake" |
| After: shoes on, take the stairs, never the elevator | American Red Cross earthquake sheet (via HHS) — https://www.peerta.acf.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/uploaded_files/3.%20Red%20Cross%20Earthquake.pdf ; Florence Civil Protection — https://en.comune.fi.it/export/sites/comune_multilingue/materiali/civil_protection/Earthquake.pdf | use stairs rather than an elevator in case of aftershocks, power outages or other damage; wear shoes, there may be glass and rubble |
| Expect aftershocks | Ready.gov, Earthquakes | "Expect aftershocks to follow the main shock of an earthquake." |

The quake itself (M 8.0, 3 a.m., rain) is fiction: a "POV" scenario, not a real event.
