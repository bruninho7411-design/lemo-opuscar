# TREATMENT — "POV: terremoto 8.0 com o seu gato"

Style: **Low-poly Isometric** · vertical 1080×1920 · 24 fps · ~40 s · pt-BR voice (VoiceStudio / OmniVoice, the young male voice from `lula-30s`, urgent and close) + burned-in captions.
Audience: short-form feeds. Tone: tense, cinematic; the cat is the heart. Goal: a POV people watch to the end, then save because it teaches the right moves (FACTS.md).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Countdown of mistakes**: "5 erros no terremoto": a list, each mistake shown then corrected. Clear, but a list kills the tension and the POV.
2. **Split before / during / after**: three tableaux of the same room. Readable, but static: a quake is motion.
3. **One room, one night, one quake (chosen)**: a cutaway apartment at 3 a.m. in the rain, seen like a model on a table. The cat wakes first, the glass trembles, the quake hits, the lights die, you take cover, the cat vanishes, silence, a phone torch and a collar bell find it, and the camera pulls back to a dark city where torches come on window by window. One place lets the audience feel the shaking; the bell gives the cat a voice; the ending turns one flat into a whole city.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Às 3 da manhã, um terremoto 8.0 atinge o seu prédio; você tem segundos para fazer a coisa certa, e o seu gato também.*

- **Setup (0–8 s):** calm, rain, warm lamp, cat asleep. The cat wakes. The water in a glass ripples. Near-silence.
- **Turn (8–24 s):** the hit. Shake, falling things, blackout. You drop under the table; the cat bolts under the sofa on its own. The window cracks. It slowly stops.
- **Ending (24–40 s):** true silence. A phone torch. A bell under the sofa: two eyes. Shoes, cat, stairs. Pull back: the dark building, torches popping on in other windows like the bell's notes. "Salva esse vídeo."

## 3. Benchmark

- **Rain-at-night POV shorts** (the user's reference frame): learn the big top caption "POV: …" held over a moody night window, slow push, ambient rain. Don't take: the realistic city, the serif face, any footage.
- **Monument Valley's calm isometric rooms** (grammar only): learn the cutaway room as a diorama, empty space, light as mood. Don't take: geometry, palette, characters.
- **Disaster films' "the glass of water" beat**: learn the insert that announces the threat through a tiny object before the big one. Don't take: any shot or score.

## 6. Cue map

- 0–7.6: low pad (bowed vibraphone drone, D minor), kalimba ostinato thinning, woodblock tick on each beat like a clock. 7.6–8.0: digital zero.
- 8.0–23.6: no melody: sub rumble (brown noise, LFO), impacts on falls, glass, a struggling pulse (soft kick at 90 bpm, irregular).
- 23.6–27.0: rain only, one drip.
- 27.0–33.6: the bell motif (cat's collar), a single celesta answering it, soft pad returns.
- 33.6–40.0: each torch = one celesta/glock note on the eighth grid, pentatonic, rising; the pad resolves to D major on the last bar.
