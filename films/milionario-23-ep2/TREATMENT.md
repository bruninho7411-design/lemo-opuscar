# TREATMENT — "POV: você ficou milionário aos 23" · EP 2 · O segredo vazou

Style: **Brick Toy** (a brick world on a real desk, shot like macro toy photography) · vertical 1080×1920 · 24 fps · ~70 s · pt-BR.
Series: EP 2 of 30. EP 1 was 2D; from EP 2 on the series moves to a brick world shot as macro photography. It looks more expensive, it is the 3D, blocky family that performs best on this account, and it holds attention at thumbnail size. The cast keeps its EP 1 colours so returning viewers recognise everyone: **Você** (dark-grey hoodie, brown hair, round glasses), **Caio** (mustard jacket, dark skin, black fade), **Seu Arlindo** (cardigan green, white moustache) and a new face, **o Homem de Terno**.
Voices: a new **narrator**, deep and dramatic, in the tone of viral story channels (OmniVoice voice design, deepest clear take). The characters keep their EP 1 voices (Você = the series voice, Caio = his designed voice). The Homem de Terno gets his own deep, older designed voice. **Music under the whole film** (a "musiquinha" bed), ducked under speech, with only two deliberate silences.
Goals: hook in the first 2 s, hold to the end, follow (mid-film and end card), come back for EP 3.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Straight continuation**: open on the sofa where EP 1 ended, Caio asks, you explain, end on a new hook. Natural, but the first seconds are a recap: viewers who never saw EP 1 scroll away.
2. **Lesson episode**: "how a laundromat makes money" told with brick charts. Shareable, but the drama stops; a series lives on what happens next.
3. **In medias res, then twelve hours back (chosen).** Open on the worst consequence: a giant real smartphone lying on the desk next to the brick town, Caio's post on screen, the view counter exploding, tiny brick-you looking up at it. "Seu melhor amigo descobriu que você é milionário. E postou." Rewind 12 hours: 3-second recap for newcomers, Caio asks, you show him the laundromat at night, the money maths in bricks (+R$ 9.000 ×3), he swears to keep the secret with his fingers crossed. Back to 7 a.m., one million views. At 8, a man in a suit waits at the door. Freeze: "EP 3 — A PROPOSTA". It opens on the strongest image, the medium's own move (toy on a real desk: the phone is landscape) carries the hook, and it ends on a new question.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Seu melhor amigo jurou guardar o seu segredo de milionário. Doze horas depois, o vídeo dele tinha um milhão de views, e um homem de terno te esperava na porta.*

- **Hook (0–6 s):** the giant phone, the post, views exploding; "E postou." Rewind.
- **Setup (6–20 s):** recap for newcomers (three laundromats on the desk, patrimônio R$ 1.000.000); Caio: "Vinte e sete mil? De onde vem isso?"; two options, lie or show.
- **Turn (20–48 s):** 11 p.m. at Lavanderia 1, lit like a jewel box on the dark desk; the maths built in bricks; Caio: "Me ensina."; follow call; "Ninguém pode saber." "Juro.", and the camera orbits behind him to show his fingers crossed.
- **Climax (48–70 s):** 7 a.m., the phone again, 1.000.000 views; 8 a.m., a black car, a man in a suit at the laundromat door; "Você é o garoto das lavanderias? Precisamos conversar." Hard cut, end card EP 3.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Brick-film stop-motion and macro toy photography**: learn the shallow focus, softbox highlights, characters on twos with a smooth camera, real objects as landscape. Don't take any characters, sets, music or brand.
- **Viral story shorts (faceless narrator series)**: learn the in-medias-res cold open, a recap under 4 s, a running number on screen, a mid-film follow line, a hard cliffhanger with the next title. Don't take any lines, music or graphics.
